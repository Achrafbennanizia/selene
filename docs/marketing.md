# SELENE — research brief & marketing strategy

Fictional **interest / briefing brand** for private cislunar seating. Numbers below are real; the product is a waitlist + education funnel, not a sold ticket.

## Verified facts (use in UI)

| Claim | Value | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Mean Earth–Moon distance | **384,400 km** (238,855 mi) | NASA / Space.com |
| Lunar apogee (approx.) | ~405,500 km | NASA |
| Apollo 11 LM site | **0.674°N, 23.473°E** · Mare Tranquillitatis | NASA NSSDC / LRO |
| Apollo transit (typical) | ~3 days to lunar orbit (Apollo 11 ≈ 76 h) | NASA |
| Earthrise photo | Apollo 8 · 24 Dec 1968 | NASA (public domain) |
| Commercial lunar tourism | dearMoon **cancelled Jun 2024** (Starship delays) | Wikipedia / Maezawa |
| Starship lunar cargo | **No earlier than 2028** · $100M / metric ton (SpaceX published) | SpaceX Moon page |
| Highest private altitude since Apollo | Polaris Dawn · **~1,400 km** (2024) — *not* lunar | Mission reporting |
| Artemis II | Crewed lunar flyby (NASA) — government crew, not tourist seats | NASA |

## Positioning

**Narrow truth:** Buyers don’t need sci-fi. They need a credible path to the first private cislunar seats — and proof we know the physics.

**Promise:** *The briefing for the first private seats past Earth orbit.*

**Not claiming:** Guaranteed 2028 tourist landings, fixed ticket price, or that SELENE operates a rocket.

## Funnel (scroll = stages)

1. **Hero** — Emotion + one hard number (384,400 km). CTA: Join the briefing list.
2. **Log** — Credibility: Apollo site coords, transit time, what “seat” means (flyby vs surface).
3. **Trajectory** — Tangible distance. Full page loads arc to completion.
4. **Missions** — Heritage frames (PD NASA) = trust, not mood boards.
5. **Reserve** — Conversion: request a seat-hold *briefing* (email + window preference). Soft CTA.

## Copy rules

1. Label industry facts vs SELENE offers.
2. Prefer “interest list / briefing” over “book your flight.”
3. One primary CTA per viewport.
4. Contrast: foam on void with scrim; never grey-on-photo without shadow.
